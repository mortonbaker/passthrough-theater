deovr-lan player

passthrough player — pick a video, then Enter Passthrough

In headset:
Right trigger — point at menu to click and drag sliders; click away from the menu to show/hide it; hold away from the menu to move it where you point
⚙ on the menu opens settings: chroma + yaw / pitch / height / zoom / distance sliders   ADV toggles gesture controls
Gestures (ADV on): right grip grab to aim (twist/tilt/lift, stick zooms), L-stick zoom & seek (grip: distance), X key strength (grip: softness), Y reset  ·  A play/pause and B exit always work
